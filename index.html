ExpressVPN Customer Service and Account Support

Account access is an important part of managing your VPN service. If you are unable to sign in to your ExpressVPN account, first check that your email address and password have been entered correctly.

ExpressVPN Support information may also be useful when dealing with password recovery or other account access concerns. Keep your password and verification information private.

ExpressVPN Support for Application Issues

ExpressVPN Support can be useful for application-related concerns. These may include installation errors, application updates, unexpected app behavior, or difficulties connecting to a VPN server.

Before seeking additional assistance, check whether the application is installed correctly and whether an updated version is available for your device.

ExpressVPN Support for Connection Problems

When ExpressVPN is not connecting or the application is behaving unexpectedly, begin with basic troubleshooting. Check your internet connection and restart the application. You can also try another available VPN server location.

  • Check your internet connection.
  • Restart the ExpressVPN application.
  • Try another available server location.
  • Check for application updates.
  • Restart your device.
ExpressVPN Support Tip: Keep your device type, operating system, application version, and exact error message available when looking for technical assistance.

ExpressVPN Customer Service for Billing
